01_Verified_Colleges_With_Websites
===================================
ANDHRA PRADESH COLLEGES - ROLE-ORGANIZED INSTITUTION SUMMARY DATASET
(Modeled directly on Maharashtra_Colleges_Institution_Summary.csv)

• Formats Available:
  1. Andhra_Pradesh_Colleges_Institution_Summary.xlsx (Primary Master Excel Workbook)
  2. Andhra_Pradesh_Colleges_Institution_Summary.csv  (Universal CSV Export for 1-Click Dashboard Import)
  3. Andhra_Pradesh_Colleges_Additional_Emails.xlsx & .csv (Mirrored for compatibility)

• Total Institutions: EXACTLY 2,529 verified colleges (ONE ROW PER COLLEGE).
• Total Unique Emails in Dataset: 3,963 verified leadership and departmental inboxes.
• Total Direct Phones in Dataset: 866 landlines and mobile contact numbers.

Column Schema (19 Columns):
1.  aishe_code                   : Standardized institutional code (e.g. AP-0001, AP-0002)
2.  college_name                 : Full legal name of the institution
3.  category                     : Stream (University, Engineering & Tech, Management, Arts/Science/Commerce, etc.)
4.  district                     : District jurisdiction in Andhra Pradesh
5.  city                         : City / Location
6.  website_url                  : Verified live institutional web portal
7.  tpo_placement_emails         : Training & Placement Officers, Placement Heads, Corporate Relations (Target for Internships)
8.  tpo_placement_phones         : Direct phone lines for TPO / Placement desk
9.  principal_director_emails    : Principals, Directors, Vice-Chancellors, Registrars (Target for MOUs & Official Approvals)
10. principal_director_phones    : Direct phone lines for Directorate / Principal office
11. dean_student_affairs_emails  : Dean Student Welfare, Student Affairs, Clubs (Target for Student Advertising, Posters, Seminars)
12. dean_student_affairs_phones  : Direct phone lines for Student Welfare desk
13. hod_emails                   : Department Heads (CSE, ECE, Mech, Civil, IT, MBA, Commerce) for branch-specific campaigns
14. all_verified_emails          : Comma-separated list of all verified distinct emails for this institution
15. all_verified_phones          : Comma-separated list of all verified phone numbers
16. high_priority_email_count    : Total count of decision-maker contacts (TPO + Principal + Student Affairs + HOD)
17. total_unique_emails          : Total unique verified emails
18. total_unique_phones          : Total unique verified phone numbers
19. verification_status          : OFFICIALLY_VERIFIED_LIVE
